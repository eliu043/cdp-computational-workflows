Chatbot as a design project

What role does it play?

In this prototype, the chatbot is an interface for inquiry. It turns a visitor's question into a direct exchange with a language model, making the prompt, wait, response, and possible failure visible. As the project develops, that role could become more specific: a guide to the project's research, a way to explore its data, or a conversational layer for explaining methods and design decisions. Its value should come from that defined context—not simply from adding a chat box.
